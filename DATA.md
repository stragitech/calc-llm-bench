# Dataset terms

The files in `data/` are University of Houston Calculus I assessment items.

Copyright stays with the University of Houston. This repository does not assign or transfer that copyright, and it does not put the items under an open license such as MIT, CC BY, or CC0.

The University authorized release of this benchmark. The items may still be in use. They are shared only so people can test language models.

## You may

- Clone this repository and run model evaluations on these files.
- Publish aggregate scores, error analysis, and a description of your method.
- Quote a few items when that is needed to explain a result, with attribution.

## You may not

- Claim ownership of the questions or the answer key, or relicense them.
- Redistribute the dataset, the answer key, or a substantial subset. That includes a new corpus, a paper appendix, a mirror, or a study guide.
- Train or fine-tune a model on these items.
- Use the items as a course, placement, or other live assessment.
- Sell the items or put them in a commercial question bank.

Aggregate metrics are fine. Copying the questions or the key into another project is not.
